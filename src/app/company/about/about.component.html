About Us

Medilife Impex Pvt. Ltd.

Medilife Impex Pvt. Ltd. was found with a mission to make homeopathic products accessible to your doorsteps with just the click of a button. We are proud pioneers and largest distributors of homoeopathic medicines in central India having an experience of 50 years.

Brand name Carmino is the face of our Premium Quality Herbal Cosmetics. All our cosmetic products are made from authentic herbal extracts and are proven to give unmatched results.

All our products are manufactured under one roof in our factory at MIDC Area, Nagpur. We are strict and ardent for practicing procedures as prescribed under “Good Manufacturing Practice” certificate. Stringent quality control is maintained by our highly experienced technical personnel in our modern state of the art manufacturing unit.

We are strongly committed to providing relentless service and high-quality products to our customers. Our greatest strength is the trustworthy patronage and confidence of our wide customer base. Therefore, we strive to achieve customer satisfaction by assigning the highest priority to the customer’s convenience, focus on the product quality, cost-effectiveness and timely delivery.
